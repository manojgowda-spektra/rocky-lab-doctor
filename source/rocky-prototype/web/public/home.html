Rocky

the reliability layer for hands-on cloud labs

engine: deterministic (AI off)

Labs rot silently. Rocky finds it, drafts the fix, and proves the fix worked.

The cloud changes underneath a lab even when nobody edits the guide — a model retires, a product is renamed, a translation drops a login token — and a learner at 2 a.m. pays for it. This demo shows the whole loop on real production guides, live, in six short acts. Everything on screen is labelled REAL or SIMULATED — Rocky never fakes green.
