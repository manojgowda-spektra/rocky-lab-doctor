# Exercise 1: Create your Foundry project

**Time:** about 5 minutes

In this lab you will deploy a real GPT-5 model and talk to it, in the real Microsoft
Foundry portal. Rocky, your lab copilot, is already in the portal with you. He flies to the
exact control you need next and glows it, and tells you *why* the step matters, not just
what to click.

> **Rocky is on the screen, not in this guide.** Follow the glow. If you want to read ahead,
> everything he says is written here too.
>
> **If a control looks different on your build**, Rocky will say so honestly instead of
> pointing at the wrong thing. Click Rocky and choose **Next** to carry on.

---

## Before you start

The lab VM opens with a desktop shortcut called **Lab Portal (with Rocky)**. It also opens
by itself about a minute after you sign in. If you closed it, double-click the shortcut.

Sign in to the portal with the lab credentials from your CloudLabs **Environment Details**
tab.

---

## Step 1 — Name your project

In the portal, type a name for your project. Rocky puts a suggested name on your clipboard,
so you can just paste it.

**Why this matters.** Every Foundry resource you create in this lab hangs off this project,
so its name is the label you will see in the portal, in the SDK and on the bill.

**What a project is.** A Foundry *project* is your working space. It holds model
deployments, data connections, agents and evaluations, and gives your team one place to
collaborate. Projects live under a *hub*, which owns the shared security, networking, quota
and connections.

**Tip.** Names must be unique in the hub and are hard to change later. Pick something short
and lowercase.

## Step 2 — Create

Click **Create**. Provisioning takes a few moments.

## Step 3 — Enter the project

Click **Let's go** to open your new project.
