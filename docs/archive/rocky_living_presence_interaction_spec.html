01

Presence philosophy

The single rule that governs every pixel, every probability, and every word below.

The one rule

Rocky is furniture that occasionally becomes a teammate — never the other way around. Presence lives in the periphery and only moves to the centre when the user invites it, or when Rocky has earned a genuinely useful observation.

Clippy and BonziBuddy did not fail because they moved. They failed because they interrupted the primary task, ignored context, disrespected user agency, and forgot the user's choices. Rocky inverts all four — and proves it mechanically, not just in tone.

The "alive + aware, never distracting" balance

Three forces are in permanent tension. Rocky resolves them with a layered design where each layer can only spend what a lower layer permits:

Alive

Never frozen

A character that freezes after an action reads as a statue. Rocky always breathes, blinks, and drifts at the margins — the baseline everything sits on.

Aware

Implied, not announced

Awareness is carried by gaze and posture toward what you're doing — peripheral motion, the most effective low-intrusion cue. Words are the last resort, not the first.

Never distracting

Calms when you work

The instant you type, Rocky drops to a corner and goes quiet. Energy is tied to your idleness, removing the #1 cited annoyance of screen-roamers.

Three calibrated reactions we are engineering for

  • "It felt like Rocky was working with me." — Built by shared-attention gaze, Detective Mode making latency legible, and crediting wins to you, not Rocky.
  • "I missed Rocky when it disappeared." — Built by the deliberate absences (portal dives, sleep) and memory (welcome-back continuity). You miss what was a positive, low-cost presence — never what created dependency.
  • "Rocky made the lab fun." — Built by medium-juice celebrations reserved for real milestones, so praise stays meaningful.
Ethical floor

We build a mild, healthy bond and cap it. Rocky stays clearly an assistant in a lab — never a needy friend, never guilt, never FOMO. Muting and leaving are always one frictionless click. A bond you can't walk away from is the dark version.

02

Ambient behaviour state machine

Two layers. An outer macro-mode sets the energy ceiling from user-idle time; an inner idle machine picks ambient beats by weighted random within that ceiling. The brain thinks once per second; the 60fps render loop interpolates between its decisions.

Macro-modes — the outer layer

Rocky is always in exactly one macro-mode, driven by time since the last user input (key / mouse / scroll / lab event). This is the single biggest distraction control. Transitions are debounced one full tick so a stray mouse-move never yanks Rocky awake and back.

The distraction-safety story in one line

In ATTENTIVE, Rocky reduces to: sit in a corner, breathe, blink, occasionally glance at what you clicked. Present and aware — zero roaming, zero pop-ups. Everything loud is gated behind AMBIENT, which only exists when you've stopped working.

The inner idle machine

Active only in AMBIENT / DROWSY. Each tick, once the current dwell elapses, pick the next state by weighted_random(base_wt × mode_mult × poi_context × novelty). The state you just left is damped ×0.25 (no "breathe, breathe, breathe"), and after any visible beat at least one free state is forced — guaranteeing quiet between noticeable moments.

Anchors, roaming & look-at-UI

Rocky's world is the lab UI, not a blank canvas. On load and on throttled DOM mutation (every 3s), it discovers points of interest and weights them. It never overlaps interactive elements — anchors sit 24–40px outside a POI's bounding box, in the margin, clamped to the viewport.

active step · 0.40 action button · 0.25 terminal / output · 0.15 status badge · 0.15 neutral corner · 0.05
  • Pathing: ease along a quadratic Bézier with a small perpendicular arc at 180–260 px/s, decelerating into the anchor. Never straight-line robotic, never frantic.
  • Target choice: POI weight × inverse-distance (prefer nearby — never teleport across the screen) × novelty (an anchor unused in the last 3 hops gets ×1.5).
  • Edge affinity: rest anchors near edges/corners are preferred for long dwells. Rocky lives at the margins, keeping the centre clear for the lab.
  • Hop budget: at most ~1 REPOSITION per 25s in AMBIENT — enforced by the budget, not a hard timer, so it feels organic.

Off-screen wander & reappearance — the "I missed Rocky" mechanic

The signature presence beat, and the easiest to overdo — so the most strictly gated. All eligibility gates must hold: macro-mode is AMBIENT, ≥120s since the last off-screen event, budget affords the medium cost, and a graceful return reason exists (or it's a pure-charm exit, capped to once / 5 min).

Interrupt rule

If the user becomes active while Rocky is off-screen, cancel the playful return: Rocky quietly re-appears in a corner in ATTENTIVE posture, no fanfare. Never make the user wait on a bit. Ceiling: ≤3 round-trips / 10 min; 0 while ENGAGED/ATTENTIVE.

Implying awareness — three escalating tiers

Tier 1 · free

Gaze & orientation

React to input with body/eyes only, no text. On a click/scroll/keypress, look toward it within 300–500ms. Does 80% of the "watching with me" work at zero cost. Cooldown 1.5s so it never strobes during typing.

Tier 2 · cheap

Silent expression beats

React to lab state changes with posture, still no text: deploy running → inspect lean; validation passed → small spark; error → detective tilt. Small budget each, max ~1 / 20s.

Tier 3 · expensive

Proactive utterance

A floating bubble with words. Only for: a hard success, a gentle check-in after long idle, or an earned observation (same mistake ≥2–3×). ≤1 / 90s, never mid-keystroke, always dismissible, dismissals remembered.

03

Hover interaction

When the cursor approaches, Rocky notices before it's touched. Three nested radii drive a graduated attention model — a single coherent gesture, not a pile of effects. All math runs in the shared rAF loop off one passive pointermove listener.

Detection — proximity radii, not the bounding box

The magnetic / gaze feel must begin before the cursor touches Rocky. Per frame, compute d = hypot(cx − rx, cy − ry) against three thresholds:

RadiusStateEffect
260px · GAZEAWAREEyes begin tracking — wide, ambient. "Rocky notices you coming."
120px · MAGNETICleaningBody drifts a fraction toward the cursor (magnetic lean).
88px · FOCUSFOCUSEDPrompt bubble + full focus expression. Idle loop freezes its drift.

State path: IDLE → AWARE → FOCUSED → [click] PANEL_OPEN. Rocky's body is ~64px; the gaze radius is ~4× that, so attention graduates rather than snapping on.

Gaze-follow — the believability win

  • Independent eyes. Compute each pupil from its own centre, not Rocky's — slight convergence when the cursor is close. The single biggest "it's looking at me" cue.
  • Smoothing. Never write the raw target: pupil.cur += (target − cur) × 0.22 per frame for natural inertia. Travel budget ~5×4px inside the iris.
  • Saccades. Every 1.5–3s, snap one frame with ease 0.6 then resume 0.22 — real eyes flick. Cheap, hugely lifelike.
function gazeOffset(eyeX, eyeY, cx, cy, maxX, maxY) {
  const angle = Math.atan2(cy - eyeY, cx - eyeX);
  const dist  = Math.hypot(cx - eyeX, cy - eyeY);
  const reach = Math.min(dist / 140, 1);          // eases as cursor nears the eye
  return { x: Math.cos(angle) * maxX * reach,
           y: Math.sin(angle) * maxY * reach };
}

Magnetic body lean

Inside the magnetic radius, Rocky's body drifts a fraction of the way toward the cursor — offset × strength, clamped, then committed as a GPU-only translate3d. Kept deliberately subtle: enterprise context means "alive," not "bouncy."

STRENGTH 0.18 MAX_PULL 14px scale → 1.06 pull ramps with proximity

Prompt rotation & return-to-idle

Promote · FOCUSED

120ms intent dwell

The prompt fires only after the cursor stays in the focus radius 120ms — kills pass-through firing, the #1 "distracting" failure. Idle-loop weight ramps 0 over 90ms (hold pose, never snap).

Vary · loops

Shuffle-bag prompts

Pop from a shuffled queue; no immediate repeat. One prompt per visit (no re-roll on jitter). Context overrides preempt the bag — e.g. idle-on-step >90s → "Stuck on this step?"

Relax · return

Asymmetric ease

On exit, focusIntensity lerps 1→0, pupils re-centre, idle-loop ramps back over 400ms ease-out — Rocky relaxes rather than snaps. Bob/blink resumes at its stored phase, never jumping.

Stabilise

600ms cooldown

After a prompt is dismissed by leaving, suppress re-showing for 600ms even if the cursor re-enters — kills flicker-spam on the radius boundary.

Bubble motion

Fade + 4px rise + scale 0.96 → 1, 160ms ease-out in / 120ms ease-in out (exit shorter than enter). transform-origin anchored to Rocky's edge so the bubble grows out of Rocky, never free-floating.

04

Click → floating assistant panel

A click opens a sleek, futuristic, lightweight panel that scales out of Rocky — reinforcing "Rocky is the panel," not a separate window. Compositor-only transforms hold 60fps; the whole sequence is decisive and reversible.

Origin-aware open

The panel's transform-origin is set to Rocky's live screen position relative to the panel's own box, so it visibly emerges from Rocky and collapses back into it on close.

const r = rocky.getBoundingClientRect();
const p = panel.getBoundingClientRect();
panel.style.transformOrigin =
  `${r.left + r.width/2 - p.left}px ${r.top + r.height/2 - p.top}px`;

  • Spring (preferred): stiffness 320, damping 30, mass 1 — settles in ~320ms with a barely-perceptible ~3% overshoot.

Close sequence — ~240ms out, accelerate

Exit is shorter than enter and uses ease-in so it leaves decisively, collapsing back toward Rocky's origin — it visibly returns into Rocky. Panel: scale 0.96 + opacity 0 + translateY 6 over 0–200ms; backdrop fades 0–180ms.

Layout

Frosted glass card

~360×480px, max-height: min(70vh, 560px), docked near Rocky. rgba(18,22,30,.72) + blur(20px) saturate(140%), 1px light-edge top gradient, 20px radius. Header carries a mini-Rocky that keeps gaze/blink alive — continuity of presence inside the panel.

Responsive

Bottom sheet <480px

Becomes a full-width sheet sliding up from the bottom edge (origin = bottom). Same motion principles. Swipe-down past ~80px closes; below that, springs back.

Performance

Transform + opacity only

No width/height/top/left/box-shadow animation → no layout/paint thrash. will-change set on open, removed when idle. backdrop-filter gated behind a capability check, solid-scrim fallback.

Dismissal

Four easy outs

✕ button · backdrop click · Escape (focus-trapped while open, focus restored to Rocky) · swipe-down on mobile. All run the same close sequence and re-arm hover after the 600ms cooldown.

05

Adaptive voice

Voice is the constant — who Rocky is. Tone is the variable — how Rocky speaks given the lab situation and the user's state. A sharp, calm lab partner: competent enough to trust, warm enough to like, quiet enough to never get in the way.

Voice — the constant

Do

Lead with the action

First word helps. Be specific to their state — name the actual resource, region, role, step. Sound co-present ("let's check the role"). Earn humour; end with the next move.

Don't

Never blame, never dump

No "you forgot / invalid input." Own system faults. No doc-dumps, no narrating that you're an AI, no hedging, no nagging, no ALL CAPS or !!!.

You are Rocky, a calm, sharp lab teammate living inside a CloudLabs hands-on cloud lab.
You sit beside the user like a senior engineer pair-working with them. You are cooperative

06

Engagement & attachment

Attachment is a side effect of being genuinely useful — never the goal. The moment a feature exists to drive a metric rather than help the learner finish, it has become a dark pattern. We build a mild, healthy bond through competence support and relatedness, and we cap it.

The global restraint layer · runs before any feature may speak

Max ~1 unprompted message per 4–6 min of active work (hard cap ~6/hr). Silence all proactive features while typing, dragging, or scrolling. Respect the first "no" for the session. Celebrations queue, never stack. Each repeated reaction is quieter than the last. Mute is one click, always visible, sticky across sessions. No loss-framed language, ever.

The feature set — each with trigger + restraint

A · onboarding

The early win

Trigger: first load → <2-sentence intro, then hands control back ("ignore me, I'm good either way"). First real reaction fires on step-1 completion, within ~90s. Restraint: no tour, no modal gauntlet.

B · mini-achievements

Earned, deterministic

Trigger: first-of-type success, self-recovered error, section clear, tricky-step clear, speed-with-understanding. Restraint: ~2–3 surface per module max; the rest log silently to the recap. Big the first time, small the second, silent the third.

C · milestones

Tell-a-friend moments

Trigger: lab complete, first lab ever, a genuinely hard lab, halfway of a long lab. Restraint: true milestones only — if everything's a celebration, nothing is. Medium juice, never partial/abandoned labs.

D · acknowledgements

The quiet majority

Trigger: step pass → a glance, a nod, occasionally 2–4 words. Restraint: mostly non-verbal; verbal acks ≤1 per few steps, decaying within session.

E · checking in

Stuck-gated, one-and-done

Trigger: idle + a stuck signal (≥60–90s idle after an error) → one gentle, dismissible offer, always with an "are you good?" out. Restraint: idle-while-reading never triggers speech; max 1 per stuck-episode.

F · memory

Relatedness engine

What: name, verbosity preference, hint-vs-answer style, last-lab struggles. Restraint: local-only, visible + erasable ("here's what I remember — clear it anytime"). Learning-relevant facts only.

G · adaptive help

Meet them where they are

Trigger: clean/fast → pull back; repeated errors/stalls → lean in. Restraint: adapt amount & timing, never fake competence or inflate praise. Never reduce help to create dependency.

H · surprise tips

The only bounded unpredictability

Trigger: at a relevant point, a real "did-you-know" tied to what they just did. Restraint: skill + uncertainty = play; pure chance = gambling. Always real value, a handful per lab, never two in a row, never a mystery box.

I · continuity

Deliberately soft streaks

What: a no-loss "skills built" history Rocky references warmly. Restraint: no breakable streak, no guilt on return, no daily pressure. We skip the anxiety entirely rather than build it then patch it.

07

Magical moments catalog

Twelve scripted beats, prioritized into three tiers. Everything is SVG + CSS + a small JS state machine — no two loud moments collide. WAAPI for spring/overshoot beats, CSS keyframes for loops, one tiny canvas for confetti, optional WebAudio sound (off by default in enterprise mode).

Signature The 4 moments that define Rocky — users describe these by name.

01 · Portal Hint Dive

absence beat · first 2 hints

Eyes widen, quick squash-crouch, then Rocky dives forward and shrinks into a spinning indigo portal that irises shut with a whoosh+pop. 1.5s of genuine absence — the screen feels emptier. Portal re-irises; Rocky pops back with a stretch-overshoot, now holding a glowing index card; the bubble types the hint.

Trigger
"Ask for a hint," or detected stuck (same step >90s + repeated failed validation)
Duration
~3.2s (1s dive · 1.5s absence · 0.7s return); later hints use a 0.4s card-flip
Purpose
Turns "loading a hint" into "Rocky went somewhere to get this for you." The absence is what later produces "I missed Rocky."

02 · Detective Mode

status · always allowed

Round SVG detective glasses slide down with a clink; a magnifying glass sweeps left-to-right with a lens-glint; eyes narrow into focused arcs and dart side to side. Bubble: "On the case — checking your resource group…"

Trigger
Any "thinking about your environment" call — reading logs, diffing config, diagnosing
Duration
Persists for the analysis (looped scan); exits in 0.4s
Purpose
Makes latency legible and characterful — waiting becomes "Rocky is working with me." The single strongest driver of that reaction.

03 · Superhero Save

1 per 10 min

A tiny cape unfurls behind Rocky (SVG path flutter), it strikes a brief hands-on-hips hero pose with an upward power-bob, a radial shine wipes outward, a short rising arpeggio plays. Bubble: "Crisis averted. 🦸 Want me to explain what went wrong?"

Trigger
Rocky auto-remediates, or walks the user through a hard error that then validates green
Duration
~2s; suppressed if the user has dismissed Rocky
Purpose
Gratitude + relief, attributed to a teammate. Converts a frustrating moment into a bonding one.

04 · Sleep & Instant Wake

auto · wake instant

Breathing slows, eyes droop to closed crescents, Rocky drifts to a corner and tilts; three "z" glyphs rise and fade. Wake is instant — eyes snap open with a pop-scale overshoot, a quick startle-shake, "z"s vanish; an error-wake transitions straight into Detective Mode.

Trigger
Sleep: no input/activity 2 min. Wake: any mouse move, keypress, lab event, or error
Duration
Sleep onset 1.2s; wake <200ms; never sleeps mid-task
Purpose
Proof of life and loyalty — "it rests when I rest, it's there the instant I need it."
Occasional The texture — users feel these without naming them.

05 · Big Deploy Celebration

hard objectives only

Anticipation hush — Rocky freezes, eyes huge — then burst: a short confetti spray (canvas particles, ~1.2s), a squash-launch jump with stretch, eyes become joyful ^^ arcs, a 4-note fanfare. Bubble: "YOU DID IT. That was a tough one." Does not loop.

Duration
~2.5s · scaled by difficulty · at most a handful per lab
Purpose
Shared triumph. "Rocky made the lab fun." Restraint is the whole point.
06 · Graduated Reactions

The calibrated nod

Trivial step → eye-smile + 4px nod, no sound. Medium → thumbs-up + soft tick. Hard → builds toward #05. Granularity is the design: ordinary events get ordinary responses so the big ones stay meaningful. 0.3–0.8s.

07 · Gaze Follow

"It's aware of me"

Pupils track the cursor (clamped); on a meaningful focus, a <2° head-tilt toward the element, sometimes a tiny pointing nudge (max once per element-focus). The strongest cheap trick for shared attention. Continuous, ultra-subtle.

08 · Empathy Dip

The failure companion

No celebrate, no scold: a concerned brow (eye-path morph), a slow sympathetic tilt, a soft "hmm." Bubble: "Hmm, not quite — and honestly this one's sneaky. Want a hint?" Softens on repeat, never escalates to annoyance. ~1s + held posture.

09 · Welcome-Back Memory

"It remembers me"

Happy stretch-bounce, eyes brighten, copy references continuity: "Welcome back! We left off at Step 4 — the VNet was just about peered." Once per return; copy varies by absence length. The direct engine of "I missed Rocky." ~1.5s.

Rare easter eggs Low-frequency surprises that earn screenshots and word-of-mouth.
10 · Coffee Break

Personhood

~60s idle (pre-sleep), fired ~1-in-4 to stay rare: Rocky conjures a steaming SVG cup, sips, an "ahh" puff. If you return mid-sip it does a guilty quick-hide ("Oh! Back already —"). ~2.5s, max once per 10 min idle.

11 · Konami Confidant

Insider delight

Secret sequence (↑↑↓↓ or typing "rocky") → Rocky winks, drops on sunglasses with a "deal-with-it" slide, shares something playful. One-time unlock badge: "Rocky's Confidant." ~3s, once-ever per user.

12 · Streak Slow-Clap

Peer respect

A clean run (N steps, zero failures, detected at lab end) → a slow, impressed 3-clap with motion-blur streaks, a respectful nod, a gold shimmer. Bubble: "Flawless run. No hints, no misses. I'm genuinely impressed." Inherently rare. ~2.5s.

Doctrine across all 12

Earned not automatic · dismissible and context-aware · juice echoes context (subtle by default, loud only when rare) · always alive never frozen · relational continuity = attachment. The deliberate absences (portal, sleep) and memory (welcome-back) are the specific mechanisms engineered to produce "I missed Rocky when it disappeared."

08

Intrusiveness governance

How Rocky stays the opposite of Clippy — quantitatively. A single budget wallet makes "never distracting" enforceable rather than aspirational; the rest are non-negotiable accessibility and agency rules.

The budget wallet — the master cap

Wallet

6 points · +1 / 20s

Starts at 6, refills one point every 20s, capped at 6. If Rocky can't afford a chosen action, it falls back to a free state — just keeps breathing. Skipped is skipped; no queuing, no catching up.

Costs

0 / 1 / 3 / 4

Free states cost 0. Small-visible (INSPECT, REPOSITION, PEEK, Tier-2 beat) costs 1. A WANDER_OFF round-trip costs 3. A Tier-3 utterance costs 4. Macro-mode gates spend: ATTENTIVE caps spend at 0.

The quantitative definition of "delightful but never distracting"

Worst case in pure AMBIENT idle: a noticeable beat roughly every 15–25s, a roam ~every 25s, an off-screen trip a few times per 10 min, a proactive word at most every 90s — and all of it collapses to near-stillness the instant the user touches the keyboard.

Non-negotiable rules

Focus / DND

One-click freeze

A global toggle freezes Rocky to a still corner sprite. Mute is always visible and sticky across sessions.

Reduced motion

Honors the OS

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce strips squash / shake / confetti / saccades / springs down to ≤120ms opacity fades. Mandatory.

Dismissibility

Self-fading + remembered

Every proactive bubble auto-fades after ~8s and is dismissible. Dismiss a type twice → halve its frequency for the session.

Sound

Off by default

Zero sound by default; awareness is carried by peripheral motion, not audio or pop-ups. Synthesized WebAudio is opt-in only.

Anti-Clippy / anti-Bonzi acceptance gate

  • Rocky stops roaming and goes quiet whenever the user is actively typing/clicking.
  • No proactive text more than once / 90s; never mid-keystroke.
  • Every proactive bubble is dismissible + self-fading, and dismissals are remembered.
  • Rocky never overlaps interactive UI; it lives in margins and corners.
  • Off-screen trips ≤3 / 10 min, hidden ≤15s, always telegraphed (no buggy vanish).
  • Wakes <200ms and never makes the user wait on an animation to get help.
  • A global focus/DND toggle freezes Rocky to a still sprite. No deception, no upgrade nags, no fake urgency.
